Recipe

Tomato, red onion and organic olive oil salad

This salad lets a few well-prepared ingredients speak for themselves.

By Bionatura·Published on 11 September 2026

Servings
2 servings
Preparation
10 min

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe tomatoes
  • 1/4 red onion
  • 2 tablespoons organic olive oil
  • A pinch of salt

Method

  1. Wash the tomatoes and cut them into wedges.
  2. Slice the red onion thinly.
  3. Combine, dress with oil and salt, then serve.

An everyday salad

Cut the tomato just before serving to keep its juices and texture.

Red onion brings a gentle contrast; adjust the amount to your taste.

Serve it at an easy pace

Let the salad rest for a few minutes so the flavours come together.

Serve it with bread, egg or a simple vegetable dish.
