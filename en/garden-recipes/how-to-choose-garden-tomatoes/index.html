How to choose garden tomatoes

A good tomato is chosen with the senses and stored with care. These pointers help you make the most of it at home.

By Bionatura·Published on 11 September 2026

What to look for

Look for unbroken skin, colour that suits the variety, and a gentle scent near the stem.

Avoid pressing too firmly: a tomato can be ripe and still be delicate.

How to keep the flavour

Keep them at room temperature if they still need to ripen, away from direct sun.

Once they are at their best, use them soon in a salad, on toast or in a simple sauce.
